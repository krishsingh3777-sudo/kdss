Before you begin

Purpose: This is a short behavioral study with three brief tasks (shape recognition, memory, and response time). It's part of a hackathon prototype, not a formal research study.

Voluntary participation: You may stop at any time by closing this tab. There is no penalty for withdrawing.

Data collected: Only your on-screen responses and reaction times (in milliseconds) are recorded. No name, email, or other identifying information is collected — you'll be assigned a random anonymous ID.

Use of data: Anonymized results may be viewed by the researcher running this prototype for demo purposes.

By checking the box below, you confirm you're 18+ (or have guardian permission) and agree to participate under these terms.
